Love/Light Index

Since the 1970s L/L Research, a small group in Louisville, Kentucky, has channeled sources who call themselves Ra, Q’uo, Hatonn, Latwii and others, and who offered a message to share with the world: that all things are one, and that we are here to learn to love.

This index gathers short excerpts from those sessions by theme, with the question that was asked where there is one. Each excerpt links to its full session on llresearch.org, where it is best read whole and in context.
