And the first step to processing that catalyst must be acceptance. Can you give us any guidance on what steps we can take, or how best to approach beginning to accept that which we perceive as unacceptable? Thank you, Q’uo.

We are those of Q’uo, and we are aware and appreciative of this query, my sister. For as we understand it, life on this planet in your current density is a complicated one that provides much challenge, much triumph, and many periods throughout your incarnation in which you feel downtrodden, unable to continue, or when you may feel uninspired. My friends, this is perfection; this is what you have chosen to use as your learning method. We do not wish to invalidate that experience, for we know it is often uncomfortable, that it is often quite confusing, and that there is much despair, especially when one looks out upon the planet and sees the pain, sees the strife, sees the warfare, the hunger, the poverty, the injustice.

From a cosmic standpoint, we can understand that pain and confusion, but we can also see the greater perspective that you are dancing along, that you are navigating your own path and finding ways to the open heart. And it is true, my sister, that acceptance is a key part of that process.

We understand how [when] seeing such injustice or issues, you may say, on your planet can be hard to find that accepting and opening the heart to that which causes pain, to that which causes anger, seems out of reach or incomprehensible, but we promise that all who are on this planet have the ability to do so. It merely takes the focusing of the faith and the will to find acceptance in these times.

We first ask that one practice that “f” word, faith. That one take time to notice what comes up within one, outside of the pain and discomfort. When one witnesses what is happening around your planet, or within yourself, what story are you telling yourself? What story are you digesting? And then look within. What is that story telling me about me? If it is an issue with a relationship, what is this other-self telling myself about myself? If it is some experience outside of self, again, what is this experience telling me about me?

All experience is yours, all joy and all pain. You are at once the villain and the victim. You are everything. When you can look upon that which causes strife, and see it as the Creator, and have faith that it is perfectly imperfect in the moment, and put energy into your faith, including through daily affirmations, then one can start to feel that faith strengthen. Repeating mantras such as “All is well” or “All is as it should be” in your daily practice, be it meditation, be it mirror gazing, or even in the simple moments of washing the dishes, or going about your other daily tasks, it will slowly but surely become part of your, how you may say, wired thinking, reprogramming that which has instilled fear, distrust, confusion, to new wiring that finds beauty, opportunity, and grace for all.

We also ask that you practice grace, that you see [the difficult moment] for what it is—a fraction of a fraction of a second in what you may see as time. That outside of this illusion, time does not exist. That outside of this cosmic play, that there is only now. And when you can see it as this finite yet infinite moment, you may find yourself feeling untethered to that [difficult] reality, finding more space to find love, to see it for what it provides you—catalyst for growth, for understanding, for opening the heart. See these moments for their opportunity, and see them as gifts. What a beautiful gift you have been given to find yourself on this planet at this time, to be a steward of the open heart, to be a shepherd to those who are in need.

And as you find yourself walking that path, though that path will almost certainly be challenging, will almost certainly have missteps, you will find that the clouds will part, that the sun will rise, and that the warmth of acceptance will bathe you.

When you can understand and genuinely feel that all is well—that nothing is cosmically wrong, and that this was a path you’ve chosen, these were lessons you decided to learn—you may see that acceptance is easier to tap into.

Going through this instrument’s memory, we see that a story was read: The Little Soul and the Sun. And the story being an expression of how all selves start as pure, innocent, as you may see them, beings who have chosen, at times, to dim their lights, to be in service to someone, that they may teach them to more purely shine their own. And then the cycle reverses, that you may pay that shine forward to an other-self, and help them accept, help them forgive. It is this positive feedback loop that you engage in whenever you walk the path of service to others. You find that the outcome increases exponentially. That when you walk with an open heart and an open hand, you find that those who walk alongside you grow. That the numbers increase. That the colors of your experience become more vibrant. And that the walk becomes a little more sure-footed.

That is not to say that you won’t encounter rainy days. But when you find pure acceptance, you’ll also find easier access to faith. For you know that once you have accepted, you see the rainbow on the other side. You will experience this, my friends. Even a simple acceptance of a small moment, or of an other-self, further opens the heart, further opens the eyes, so that you know, you see, you feel closer to unity, and that the pathway to acceptance is easier to navigate because it becomes more part of your being. You recognize that the patterns that you have engaged in that do not allow for acceptance no longer suit you; that they no longer make sense for the ease of beingness, the true flow of love through you from the Creator that is more deeply felt through acceptance.

We understand that there is also space for judgment, for you are still bound to your third-density bodies. You still see yourself as separate from others. We know that that can be a roadblock to acceptance. For through this separation behind the veil, you see yourselves as in at times a survival mode. That there is something to covet, or cherish, or protect.

My friends, your heart does not need protection. It needs expression. That is how it fully thrives. How light and love can more vibrantly exude from within you and outward.

Trust that your heart is pure. Trust that your heart knows what unity is; and as you trust that, feel the release of that which binds you to your identity, to that which tells you that you need to fear, or be uncomfortable. My friends, this does not preclude actions that keep you momentarily safe, you may say. We understand the drive for a home, access to food and water, keeping those who have hurt you from you, and those are valid. But it is the expression of genuine love towards those things, in balance with the wisdom to protect yourself, which is a greater expression of this topic of acceptance.

But knowing always the undercurrent is moving you forward. It cannot be stopped. It simply moves in other directions, perhaps down a stream with some extra rocks and bends. Perhaps down a stream that is faster flowing. There is a confluence at the end, where you realize oneness. Accept the path you are on, love it, forgive it, understand it. Meditate upon it, and see it for the bounty that it is. View your other-self as this wonderful gift. View every opportunity as pregnant. Feel it within you, and know its perfection. And give yourself grace and patience, knowing that you are trying your best, and knowing that the universe sees you trying your best. My friends, acceptance is perhaps the hardest lesson here.

It is a great step forward, but one that is won through much strength training, you may say, much practice. Meditation, a simple expression of love when perhaps it wasn’t necessarily expected or warranted, walking with a smile on your face, waving to those that you know and may not know, seeing the beauty of nature, finding that inner sanctum, whatever it may look like, where you may sit with the Creator and understand that all is ultimately well, will be of the greatest service to you. Your intuition will guide you. Trust it. Court it. Make friends with it. And soon, acceptance will be but a memory. For you will be on another journey, further along, walking alongside us, and that which once separated you and told you that you needed to have fear will be long behind you, and you’ll bask again in the Sun of the One Infinite Creator, and the universe will rejoice in that moment.

Again, we are those of Q’uo, and we have appreciated speaking on this topic of acceptance, and we wish you well as you continue to find that which serves you in that purpose.

Yes, Q’uo, this question comes from one seeker here, although it applies to all seekers here. I have been confronted lately with situations in which someone wants more from me than I am willing to give, such that when I say no or otherwise hold my boundaries, they are in pain. I don’t want to cause them pain, and I’m doing my best to be of service while also taking care of myself and maintaining authenticity. I am experiencing some confusion about how best to navigate this and be of service in this type of situation. I don’t think it would be of service to disregard my own authentic boundaries and give what they ask for.

Can you please speak to this and help me understand how to best be of service in this type of situation?

We are those of Q’uo, and we are aware of and appreciate this query, my brother. We find this an interesting second query to the first. We can understand how there is confusion there, how there’s much pain, for we know that deep within, your drive is in service to others. But my friends, there is much service to self that is inherently service to others. The tending to of the self, the moments of self-care, of grace, and of knowing the self.

Therefore, we understand that boundaries are important in this density, though they may be implemented with the purity of love, authentic love, and acceptance that we spoke of in the previous query.

To understand how to set boundaries while exercising love, one may meditate upon the other-self who is overstepping what you see as a boundary, or infringing upon you in ways that you wish to set up a boundary. See them for their full self, not the self that they are now, but the version of their self that is the Creator. Imagine them as the Creator. Feel them as the Creator. And understand that, as they came upon this planet, they allowed baggage, costume, identity, to hinder their light, [in order] to help the Creator know Itself more fully, that you have may have these experiences which teach you more about yourself.

I love you, but I cannot be of service to you in this specific way that you ask.” And paradoxically, my friend, in doing so, you are providing a service to them. You are providing them the catalyst that they may need, that they may use to learn about themselves.

Perhaps your boundary is a springboard in which they can further realize that which they need, and that which they can self-source. Perhaps it is a moment where they can reflect, where they can find their trauma, and where they can see how they may heal themselves.

We know that relationships are ultimately some of the greatest catalysts you can have on this planet, and so when you have these moments where you are establishing a boundary, please understand that that is what you are here to do in this life. You are here to find the balance between love and wisdom. Ultimately, you are here to open your heart. But opening your heart can come with some wisdom so that you do not become a martyr to this service.

And in that balance, you may discover a fuller version of yourself, an empowered version of yourself, a self that you now know more clearly, that you love more deeply; and when you love yourself and accept yourself more deeply, you can, from afar, love and accept that other-self more deeply. And over time, you may find that that boundary can slip away. But it is okay if it doesn’t. There will be many opportunities to learn that lesson, and that is not to say that you aren’t striving to graduate to earn the grade. But even boundary setting, when done with a loving intention, without judgment, can “score you those points.”

So as you sit and you ponder these dynamics, as you evaluate whether you should continue in service to that other-self by standing in the moment, or by exercising distance, remember that you cannot make a misstep. Ultimately, you may fumble, but again, your walk is still forward. Your path may divert, but it does not go backwards. So long as you trust your heart, that you open your heart, and that you allow the love and light of the Creator through you, you will be guided. See the signs. Ask for support from us, from the universe, from the Creator, from other-selves. You will not be left alone.

You will find that support, and that other-self will find support as well. For no soul here is truly alone. No soul here is truly lost. You are simply navigating what is a very confusing, confusing landscape—one that sees you as separate from one another. Know that, as the song sang, you are here just for a moment, and then you fly. Take advantage of this moment, and utilize it for that which serves the ultimate good, and serving the ultimate good can be respecting your selfhood.

My friends, please do call upon us in moments when you feel unsure. Call upon your higher self. Trust that all will be well. There is no being or higher power in this universe that will damn you or shame you. This is a loving creation that sees you as an innocent child, and that simply wants to hold your hand and shine the way towards unity and oneness again, towards peace, towards comfort, towards that bliss; and when you call upon us, know that we are there. For we are always in your hearts. You cannot escape that love. It is part of your DNA, physically, mentally, emotionally, and spiritually. You are the Creator. You are always creating creation.

We are those of Q’uo, and we are again so thankful and appreciative to speak to this balance of love and wisdom that you are attempting to make sense of, and at this time we would transfer our contact to the one known as Gary. We are those of Q’uo.
