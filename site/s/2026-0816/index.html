Yes, can you define and correlate the concepts of boundaries, protection, and sovereignty, as they relate, interrelate to the issues of emotional, psychic, and astral interference greeting, both from other selves and non-physical entities? And can you offer healthy ways and disciplines for an awakening heart/mind to remain safe and connected to themselves and their mental/spiritual well-being throughout their journey toward unity?

Q’uo

We are those of Q’uo, and we are aware and appreciate the query, my sister. As you dance in this third-density illusion, it may seem that you are separate entities, that you have separate experiences, that there is uniqueness to each and every one of you. And in that way, you may see yourself as atoms bouncing off one another to create this matter, to create this dense tapestry of experience. And so we understand how there may be moments where one feels infringed upon, one feels as though their presence or physical/emotional/mental/spiritual space has been encroached upon, has been affected.

Using the term “greeting” is quite powerful in that respect. For, from our experience where we see all as one, that which can be felt as a push of the boundary is simply an expression of the Creator greeting another expression of the Creator. To view oneself outside of boundaries, to see oneself as yet another portion of Creator experiencing Creator, one may feel empowered to understand that the only infringement is that which separates. To see infringement as something that is an injury or an assault is separating the self from all of creation.

Now we say that fully understanding that there are moments in this third-density illusion where there are harmful experiences, painful experiences, catalysts between self and other-self, be it on this plane or outside of this plane, that feels harmful, that feels like an invasion, that can cause trauma, and we would not invalidate that experience. For that experience is fruitful in terms of seeking acceptance and forgiveness for self, for other-self, for finding healing for that trauma, for growing, learning, and understanding the self and the Creator more fully, and to finding ways to patch that hurt part of one’s soul so that the open heart may be opened even further.

In the larger sense of creation, where there is no veil, where there is no separate I, separate you, there are no boundaries to push. There is no protection needed. For all is Creator. All are the atoms, seemingly, a part of one another. For there is no bumping. And so, in those moments where you feel infringed upon, where you feel as though protection is needed, we would say to you that that experience is valid in this illusion. However, we would say to empower oneself is to see oneself as imbued with the perfection of the Creator, and that the experience is inherently imbued with the perfection and love and light of the creation.

When you feel as though something, someone has invaded your well-being, has negatively impacted you in a way that causes imbalance, we would stress to you that focusing on love and light, acknowledging this as an experience potent for learning about the resilience of oneself, of inhaling that which is the fullness of creation and exhaling acceptance may be powerful, may be useful to finding forgiveness and acceptance and understanding for this confusing place.

We cannot speak to how this may change beyond your plane of existence, but the core result, the core knowing of this experience that transcends all densities of this experience is that you are empowered, perfect, and intentional. That you are strong. That all is well, and when you can more fully sit in that realization, in that truth, my friends, nothing, nothing can bring you down, can chip away at your perfection, can dirty the mirror that is the window to your soul. Nothing can sully your reputation or cheapen you in your value.

We stress that that is true, regardless of whether you realize this truth. We simply state that standing in it, planting your feet firmly in that knowing, even if faking it until you make it at first will help one establish an empowered position where one is not a victim to circumstance. But rather, one is walking a path confidently, ready to face that which may be a challenge, ready to accept that which may feel like an infringement, with open heart, open arms, and a curiosity to understand what is there to mine from this lesson. What gem is there to uncover about my reality, which speaks and informs the Creator of the larger creation.

We know that this is not an easy task, and we hope that our words do not make this task sound as though it is not hard fought. My friends, you have all the power within you, and you have all the support of all of creation behind you. When you desire to feel that support in your moments of challenge, know that you can always call upon us, your friends of Q’uo. We are there to support you. For this universe is inherently a supportive one.

We would end this response by stating that your experience in this incarnation is a short one, as you see time. There will be many opportunities to test your resiliency, to learn more about yourself, to develop and hone your compassion, your open-heartedness. You’ll have many moments where you feel as though things could have gone differently, as though you have not felt safe, as though you’ve felt imprisoned or caught up in some kind of negative catalyst or some outward influence that feels outside of you. Perhaps the most simple and yet challenging way of viewing this is seeing this incarnation as a bit of a roller coaster. It is short. It is at times challenging and frightening. It is at times thrilling. Take advantage of each second with open eyes. See each hill and valley with excitement, with wonder, with acceptance that you cannot stop this ride forward, and there will be an end point. Know that each moment is perfect.

Accept those within your heart that challenge you, and you will more fully understand creation. Find that genuine, pure point in yourself that feels yourself imbued within other-self. Be that physical self, be that astral self, be that spiritual influence. You are a part of that greater being. Feel that reflection within yourself and find forgiveness and acceptance, and, my friends, nothing will disempower you again.

We are those of Q’uo, and again we appreciate this opportunity to speak on this topic. And we would ask if there is another query to which we may speak?

Yes, can you speak to the spiritual nature of family relationships, specifically parents and children? How do we best analyze this unique relationship for spiritual revelation?

©2026 by L/L Research (Louisville, KY) · this turn on llresearch.org

Q’uo

We are those of Q’uo, and we are aware and appreciative of this query, my sister. The relationship between the household, biological or non-biological family, parent and child can be one of the most pregnant with possibility dynamics. It is a great opportunity for self to be a reflection of other-self in close quarters in everyday contact.

It is a strange and sometimes confusing space to exist. For it is one where one entity may see the other grow and deteriorate, while the other sees the other grow and strengthen. It is especially unique in that there is this life-giving quality, this investment of sexual and intentional loving energy to create beingness from seemingly nowhere.

That is a most powerful force, that life can generate life through love. And we know that sometimes the biological process of life creation may not inherently involve love in terms of a conscious decision, but, my friends, before you came to this incarnation, there was a loving and intentional agreement made between parent and child that this situation, that this experience would be fostered to generate catalyst to learn more about Creator, to experience more of that unique tapestry of life, as you call it.

We understand that there are those on this planet who incarnated in certain familial situations that were incredibly challenging, traumatic even, abusive, violent. Those are incredibly valid, and those were paths chosen by many of you, who wish to be challenged in a way that sparked ultimate compassion and understanding. That is one of the most challenging lessons one may take on, and that speaks to your strength. That speaks to your desire to more fully crystallize and hone the ability to love unconditionally, to open the heart more fully, even at the expense of well-being.

At times, that can result in the martyr’s path, and at times, it can generate a most beautiful blossoming both for child and for parent, for abused and abuser. Because again, an agreement was made, however seemingly outside of reality it may seem in this incarnation.

And we understand that there are those on the other side who incarnated in a most loving, accepting, beautiful family, where parent and child get along like best friends, like soulmates, and in those situations as well, there is much catalyst. The initial part of the incarnation may seem like smooth sailing, but the greater catalyst comes later in life. Perhaps when child must tend to parent as they age and deteriorate and must say goodbye as they transition. Or perhaps in situations where the child transitions before the parent, and the parent must then undergo a great sense and a great journey of loss and grief, of understanding how this could happen.

All of these potentials are made more complex, the catalyst more concentrated due to the fact that there is shared DNA. Due to the fact that the agreements were made many lifetimes ago. Due to the fact that your shared space, your shared name, at times, only hastens and accentuates the love, the anger, the pain, the healing.

Those of you who walk this incarnation with a broken heart or pain or baggage due to the parent-child relationship, we ask that you lovingly tend to that hurt child. That you approach that inner child with the same grace that a most loving and accepting parent would. That you treat that inner child in the way that you wish you had been treated, with full love and acceptance, with patience and grace, with understanding, and of course, always with affirmations of perfection.

Those of you who are parents who have experienced pain in the relationship with the child, we simply ask that you continue to strive to be open-hearted and accepting, to witness the child where they are, to see them in their imperfect perfection, and to learn to love them unconditionally regardless of that pain, regardless of disappointment or of unmet expectations. And if they continue to walk away from the love that you give, that you not weaken that love for an instant. Though they may not feel that love from afar, consciously, at a soul level, you are tending to them. You are acting upon that agreement that you made.

Those of you who are about to embark upon the path of parenthood, we would humbly suggest meditating upon that which you feel is most important in parenthood: be that the open heart, the full acceptance, the unconditional love, the ability to tend when there is hurt, but also the freedom for that which springs from you to explore, to learn, the gentle guidance, the tending to of curiosity and fostering of wonder, and the full gratitude for this expression of the Creator that you, as an extension of the Creator, created.

My friends, it is a most beautiful expression of the Creator, and for those of you who may not have the opportunity to experience this from the position of the parent, we would humbly suggest that there are many opportunities for you to exercise that role in other ways: the tending of plants, of animals, of the home, of friends, of families and your careers, and most importantly, of self. The full and generous tending to of the soul, of fostering that which speaks to you, that aids you to be the most complete version of yourself as you see yourself, though you are already complete.

As you navigate these dances in your spiritual households, know that parenthood is not only biological. There are chosen families. There are those who come into your life who are even more important to your learning than those who may have been designed as your parents or agreed upon as your parents. The same goes for you, parents, that there may be friends along your path who are not your children, but who are bigger parts of your journey.

And so, to say that the relationship between parent and child or brother, sister, husband, wife, and so on, has a hierarchy would be untrue. For once you leave this veiled experience, these roles fall away, and you are simply one with all. You are not mother. You are not father. You are not child, brother, sister, husband, wife, partner, friend. You are just you. You are all. Invite each experience and relationship with the loving care of an open heart, regardless of what that role looks like, regardless of how you identify that role, and you may find the nurturing, cherished experience of the stereotypical parent or the child. Simply see all of creation as perfect, as worthy, with patience, and you too shall feel that in return.

My friends, we are so appreciative to be able to talk through these dynamics with you from our position, where we are simply a few steps ahead. We share our insight humbly, for we know that experience can be multifaceted. We can only speak from ours, and so we hope that we have been able to assist in some way.

And at this time, we shall leave this contact and transfer the contact to the one known as Gary. We are those of Q’uo.

Q’uo

We are those of Q’uo, and we hear and receive this question from you, our internally noisy friends. We understand the difficulty when that thought stream is roaring and speeding forward rapidly, each thought with its siren song of separation, each thought clamoring to hold and to capture your attention, that your attention looks not back to source but is preoccupied with the various stories of roles and costume, achievements and losses, injuries incurred and desires of a separate being, living among other separate beings seeking its separate aims.

We understand that the braking mechanism to this momentum may seem to be out of reach, or, if discovered for a moment, may escape the inner eyes as the next thought arises and the next and the next such that there is forgetfulness.

We understand this calling for silence. For this silence is where we abide and find our beingness in the eternity of now. And limited though our capacities are from our dimensional variance, we would be happy, our brothers and sisters, to stand alongside you upon the path and send, shall we say, a steadying vibration to your focus and your presence if you would be with us now in a moment of silence.

[pause for silence]

We feel your full beingness, whatever your manner of expression. Whether you are clouded with thought, or whether you have disciplined the attention such that you may rest in stillness and silence relatively empty of thought. We see your fullness, but we embrace you particularly in the silence, our friends. For that is where our hearts meet yours.

We cannot, shall we say, inhabit your soul, be, or occupy your mind/body/spirit complex and stop the thoughts for you or rather divest your awareness from identification with thought. But we can assist your own efforts, and we do every time we are called. We seek to strengthen your efforts. We seek to stabilize your seeking. We, shall we say, model silence for you from silence to your silence. That silence, that stillness is within you always.

The journey is to release the entrancement, the hypnosis of the mental stream of the identification with a body and an individual “I” that you may perceive this undergirding silence; that you may allow that silence to speak to you; that you may subject this busy, outward-going, resisting, separate entity to that realm where there is no content, no doing, no way to be a separate entity; that the opaque, caked-on layers of separation built of lack of forgiveness, lack of acceptance, resentment, hatred, jealousy, envy, and so forth [may fall away to reveal the truth of what is].

These things, perfect though they are in their time, layer over this crystalline, gem-like, window-like, transparent vessel that you are. Silence and stillness, and the love and forgiveness that flows from this source, is as a fire which burns away the dross of the separate self that separates the wheat from the chaff, revealing to the self that which is illusory and that which is true.

For beings such as you seem to be, our friends, so thoroughly caught in this game, it is so fundamentally important to hit pause, to step away from not only the outer commitments, entanglements, and momentum but to learn, through gentle but consistent discipline, to pull back the awareness from the thoughts themselves that circulate ceaselessly in your mind.

You and many of your planet are so accustomed to a stream of thought that has no gap, except in those rare moments when beauty or truth or love strikes you so powerfully that the mind is arrested, thought is suspended, and you are fully present. Trauma may have this effect as well and may crack open the hard shell of the self that presence may be tasted and new light may enter the system, though with great healing and repair necessary to follow.

Few are the gaps in your thought stream, but we assure you, our friends, that awareness is before and infinitely greater than any thought form. Thoughts are phenomena [that arise] within boundless, eternal awareness. And even for the third-density entity, such as you are, with your biological wiring, such as it is, through disciplined work it is possible to be with silence and stillness in such a way that you may rest in the present moment in awareness without thought. Only simple, naked awareness.

This is not to make thought an enemy or to suggest that the third-density entity ought to strive to banish these transient fragments of the Creator. Rather, it is simply to encourage the third-density entity to discover true self beyond name and form, beyond thought. And that comes by way of the paradoxical effort and effortlessness, that exercise of the will in faith, and the learning to surrender the personal will to the Creator, to the One, to rest in what is with love and acceptance, trusting that Author, capital A, to write the script, shall we say, to see to the many things. In this resting, that infinite heart that it may be through you; it may speak through you; light may pass through your system, through your voice, your eyes; it may embrace all others and shine into their own self-created darknesses of separation.

Spend time in silence, our friends. This is one of the most powerful and effective tools that you have for that change that you seek. The change of releasing what you are not in order that what is may reveal itself to you.
