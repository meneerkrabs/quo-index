Within the overall span of fourth density itself, this is but a tiny portion of that period of time. However, relative to your experience of time, it could be some decades before this harvesting process is complete.

The determination of which souls are available for incarnation in order to attempt the harvest is that which is overseeing, in a sense, by entities from the Confederation of Planets in the Service of the One Creator, such as are we. This is not, shall we say, a strict hands-on operation, but is that which is simply the observing of those entities who have placed themselves, by their own progress, at the door of incarnation, may we say. This progress is measured by a kind of light vibration which those souls who are capable of achieving harvest are able to pass by moving through the filtering light that is congruent with a certain amount of the green-ray energy center being able to welcome and enjoy this light.
